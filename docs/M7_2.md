# M7.2 — SPICE-Level Electrical Validation

## Objective

Validate the critical electrical behavior of the M6.7 hardware architecture with deterministic
reference calculations and SPICE-compatible netlists.

## Simulated blocks

1. DO TIA: 47 kOhm / 100 nF feedback network.
2. pH AFE: 1.65 V bias + high-Z electrode + differential ADC representation.
3. NH3 input: 1 kOhm / 100 nF ADC-side filter with 100 kOhm input load.
4. Battery monitor: 10 kOhm / 3.3 kOhm divider across a 6.0-8.4 V pack.
5. Power budget: 5 V @ 1 A, 3.3 V @ 0.8 A, 4 V @ 2 A sensitivity over 85/90/95% efficiency.
6. GSM transient capacitor sizing using dV = I*dt/C.

## Results from reference simulation

- DO TIA feedback pole: ~33.86 Hz; time constant 4.7 ms.
- At 3 uA probe current, ideal TIA output is 141 mV.
- pH Nernst slope at 25 C: ~59.17 mV/pH; pH 4 and pH 10 are approximately +177.5 mV and -177.5 mV relative to the 1.65 V bias.
- NH3 ADC filter: 1.59 kHz idealized with only the 1 kOhm series resistor; about 1.61 kHz with the modeled 100 kOhm ADC load (parallel-loading effect); ~99–100 us time constant.
- Battery divider maps 8.4 V to about 2.08 V and 6.8 V to about 1.69 V.
- Selected maximum rail loads total 15.64 W before conversion losses.
- The GSM capacitor table is a sizing aid only; it does not prove that a chosen capacitor or regulator combination passes a 2 A transient.

## Important limitations

The repository does not contain an ngspice/LTspice binary or vendor macromodels for every selected part.
Therefore M7.2 includes a deterministic analytical reference solver and SPICE-compatible netlists,
not a claim of transistor-level SPICE execution.

The AD8607, LMR51430, CN3722, ADS1115 and SIM800 interfaces should be re-run with vendor macromodels
and exact passive/PCB parasitics before fabrication sign-off.
