# PondGuard M7.4 — Worst-Case & Transient Refinement

## Scope
Refines the M7.3 electrical flags using reduced-order transient models that include capacitor ESR, finite regulator current-loop response, battery/source resistance, converter efficiency, and parameterized BMS current limits.

## Modeling boundary
No manufacturer LMR51430 SPICE macromodel was executed in this environment. The transient engine is a deterministic reduced-order boundary model, not a vendor-certified closed-loop SPICE result. The included netlist explicitly marks the idealized regulator source that should later be replaced by a vendor macromodel if final sign-off requires it.

LMR51430 is treated as a 3 A architecture capacity bound. The model does not use 3 A as a claim about its transient-loop response.

## GSM rail
The SIM800 rail is modeled at 4.0 V with a 2 A, 1 ms transmit burst. The sweep includes bulk capacitance, capacitor ESR, and a first-order regulator current-response time constant.

The important design metric is the **robust minimum capacitance**: the smallest candidate value whose modeled minimum rail remains at or above 3.4 V across every swept ESR and regulator-response case. The present reduced-order sweep gives **3.3 mF** as the first robust passing candidate; **4.7 mF** provides additional margin.

## Battery path
The M7.3 15.64 W simultaneous-rail case is retained and evaluated from 6.0–8.4 V, 85–95% efficiency, 50–250 mΩ source resistance, and 3–6 A parameterized BMS continuous ratings.

A new check evaluates **battery terminal voltage under load**, not just current. At the 6.0 V pack point, the worst source-resistance cases can pull terminal voltage below the 6.0 V low-voltage boundary. This means a BMS current rating alone is not sufficient to guarantee operation at the existing 6.0 V design point; low-battery load shedding/cutoff must be defined after the actual pack and BMS are selected.

## Acceptance criteria
- GSM Vmin >= 3.4 V for the modeled 2 A / 1 ms pulse across the selected sweep domain.
- Battery current <= selected BMS continuous rating.
- Battery terminal remains above the selected low-voltage boundary under modeled source resistance, or the system explicitly sheds load before that point.
- Results depending on unselected hardware remain flagged as engineering assumptions.
