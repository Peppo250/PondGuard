# M6.1 — Power Subsystem

## What changed

M6.1 performed a datasheet-level consistency check of the previously proposed
power chain and found an important incompatibility:

`CN3791 + 2S battery` is not a valid direct charger pairing because CN3791 is
a single-cell charger.

A second compatibility issue follows from the candidate CN3722 replacement:
CN3722's specified input range begins at 7.5 V, so a 6 V nominal PV module
cannot be assumed to drive that charger correctly.

## Decision

The power architecture is now represented as:

```text
PV module (compatible Vmp)
       ↓
protection
       ↓
CN3722
       ↓
2S Li-ion + 2S BMS
       ↓
7.4 V nominal / 8.4 V full
       ├── 5 V buck
       │     └── GSM regulator + other 5 V loads
       └── 3.3 V regulator
             └── ESP32 + analog/sensors
```

## Vendor-verified inputs used for this correction

- CN3791: single-cell Li-ion charger, 4.2 V regulation, 4.5–28 V input.
- CN3722: supports single- or multi-cell Li-ion, 7.5–28 V input, programmable
  CV using an external divider.
- MP2307: 4.75–23 V input, up to 3 A; manufacturer now marks it not
  recommended for new designs.
- SIM800L: 3.4–4.4 V supply, recommended 4.0 V, up to 2 A transmit bursts.

## Calculations

For the existing 7.4 V / 4.4 Ah battery concept:

`E ≈ 7.4 × 4.4 = 32.56 Wh`

For a 5 W source, an ideal upper-bound charging current at an 8.4 V battery
voltage is:

`I ≈ 5 / 8.4 ≈ 0.60 A`

This is only an energy/current upper-bound calculation; real charging current
depends on PV operating point, charger losses, battery state, temperature,
and available sunlight.
