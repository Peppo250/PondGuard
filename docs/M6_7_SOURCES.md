- Espressif ESP32-C3 hardware design guidelines: GPIO2/GPIO8/GPIO9 strapping pins; GPIO18/GPIO19 USB-JTAG defaults.
- Texas Instruments LMR51430: active 4.5–36 V, 3 A synchronous buck, 0.6 V feedback reference.
- Texas Instruments ADS1115: active 16-bit, 4-channel I2C ADC; 2.0–5.5 V supply; ADS1115IDGSR active package option.
- SIMCom SIM800 Hardware Design V1.10: 3.4–4.4 V supply, 4.0 V recommended, up to 2 A TX burst, local bulk/ceramic bypass and 5.1 V clamp.
- Consonance CN3722: 7.5–28 V input, PV MPPT, multi-cell Li-ion charger controller.
- Monolithic Power Systems MP2307: marked NRND / not recommended for new designs; retained only as legacy reference.
