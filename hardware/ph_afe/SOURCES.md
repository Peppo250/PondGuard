# M6.3 design references

1. Analog Devices CN0326 — Isolated Low Power pH Monitor with Temperature Compensation
   https://www.analog.com/en/resources/reference-designs/circuits-from-the-lab/CN0326.html
   Used for: AD8603 pH buffer, high source impedance context, guarding/shielding,
   output filtering, two-point calibration.

2. Analog Devices AD8603/AD8607/AD8609 datasheet
   https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/ad8603_8607_8609.pdf
   Used for: AD860x family specifications and dual-channel AD8607 selection.

3. Texas Instruments AN-1852 — Designing With pH Electrodes
   https://www.ti.com/lit/an/snoa529a/snoa529a.pdf
   Used for: bipolar pH output, single-supply level shifting, high-impedance
   buffering, temperature dependence.

4. Texas Instruments ADS1115 product/datasheet
   https://www.ti.com/product/ADS1115
   Used for: 16-bit ADC, differential inputs, PGA and I2C interface.
